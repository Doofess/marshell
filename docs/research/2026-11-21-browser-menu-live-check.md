## Findings

1. **The Browser menu was squeezed against the window's right edge.** It was 188 px wide, its labels wrapped and ran over each other. The menu was sized by the room beside its anchor. Fixed in `0588a071`: the menu is as wide as its rows and no wider than the window (`ContextMenu.css.test.ts`, "sizes the menu by its content, not by the room beside the anchor").
2. **In a menu with check marks the labels were pushed to the row's end, and a reason sat beside its label.** Fixed in `ad895cf7`: labels start at the row's start, a reason or a note takes a line of its own under the label and doesn't widen the menu (`ContextMenu.css.test.ts`, "starts every label at the row's start", "keeps a reason and a note on a line of their own"). Both fixes change every context menu, not only this one.
3. **The palette put "Save full-page screenshot" before "Save screenshot" for the query `Save screenshot`.** Fixed in `973041b8`: a query that occurs whole is one unbroken match, and each extra run costs points (`filter.test.ts`). Seen live afterwards: "Save screenshot" comes first for `Save screenshot` and for `save screen`. This changes every launcher and palette ranking.
4. **A cookie set in the last half minute is lost when the app is killed.** Stopped by PID 25 s after `/set-cookie` under "Keep always", the next run sent no cookie; after a normal quit it did. The shell's quit closes the pages first for this reason. Not fixed: a kill can't be caught.
5. **A switch of the cookie rule loads the front tab twice.** The site saw the front page requested twice within 150 ms on a switch to or from "Never keep". Harmless here; a page that posts on load would post twice. The page store asked the shell for the front tab's page a second time while its first ask was in flight, and the shell takes a second open of a key it has as a navigation. Fixed after the check in `4901cbf1` (`pageStore.test.ts`, "asks the shell once for a page it is already asking for at the same address"); the fix was not seen in the running app.
6. **Under "Never keep" a cookie lives until the tab's page goes.** A cookie set under "Never keep" was sent on the next request in the same run and was gone after the restart. That is what the rule promises, and the note records it so nobody reads "never" as "not even in memory".
7. **Full-page pictures are 1425 px wide at Desktop, not 1440.** The 15 px are the page's scrollbar. Not fixed.
8. **The chip cut its own text** ("Desktop 1440 …"). Its width was capped at 16 characters and "Desktop 1440 · 34%" has 18, so the cut didn't depend on the toolbar's width. Fixed after the check in `e708eea9` (`ViewportChip.css.test.ts`, "is wide enough for the longest label"); not seen in the running app.
9. **"Close session" from the palette seemed to close nothing; it works as designed.** A session whose agent process is running asks first, in a bar above its terminal (`role="alert"`, "Cancel" and "Close now"), not in a dialog. The check looked for a dialog and missed the bar. Nothing changed.
10. **After the first auto-open, focus was once found on the "Files" toggle.** The window had no OS focus at the time, and three later runs kept focus in "Terminal input". Not reproduced.
11. **The "Marshell menu" button in the title bar does nothing when pressed.** It has `aria-haspopup="menu"` and had no handler; older than this slice. Fixed after the check in `38b8b6ff`: it opens the app menu under itself (`MainWindow.test.tsx`, `appMenu.test.ts`); not seen in the running app.
12. **Dev only: after a restart the window stays blank for about 45 s.** The dev server answered no request on any address for that long, the app's included, and then served all at once. A release build has no dev server.

The console showed only the favicon's 404 and the block of scripts in a sandboxed HTML file, which is the sandbox at work.
