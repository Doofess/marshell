- Date: 2026-11-19, 21:13 to 22:54 local time.
- Build: `phase-6q-6` at `a2cc51f3` under `pnpm tauri dev` on Windows 11 Pro (10.0.26300). The eight fixes below reached the running app through Vite's reload. A tab's notes editor is made when the tab opens, so every recheck ran in a tab opened after its fix. The quit-and-start check restarted the app once, with the same scratch homes.
- Home: a scratch `MARSHELL_HOME`, `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, `CODEX_HOME`, `COPILOT_HOME`, `USERPROFILE` and `MARSHELL_CLAUDE_SETTINGS`, with CDP on port 9333. Onboarding skipped every agent's consent, so no hooks were installed. Both sessions were plain PowerShell ("Just the shell").
- Data: PDFs written by two Python scripts (pypdf, and PDF objects written by hand), outside the repo. Each was copied into the check's folder before use, so a save never touched an original.
  - `text3.pdf`: three Letter pages of pangram lines.
  - `rotated.pdf`: page 1 has `/Rotate 90`, and page 2 is landscape.
  - `acrobat.pdf`: an Acrobat-style highlight with a comment ("Check this date"), a "Sign here" FreeText, and a Square, each with an appearance stream.
  - `enc-noannot.pdf` (RC4-128, no annotation permission) and `aes256.pdf` (AES-256, annotations allowed).
  - `signed.pdf` (a `/Sig` value with a placeholder `/ByteRange [0 0 0 0]`), `signed-valid.pdf` (the same with a real `/ByteRange` around its `/Contents`) and `blanksig.pdf` (an empty signature field).
  - `scanned.pdf` (page 1 has no text) and `big.pdf` (47 MB).
  - Copies under other names (`rf-1`, `sel-1`, `chg-*`, `del-1`, `rs-*`, `loss-*`, `disk-*`) for the checks that change or delete a file. `readonly.pdf` had its read-only attribute set. A PowerShell `FileStream` held a write lock, with share Read and once with share None.

  The files were opened with `marshell open "<path>"` typed into the session's terminal. Saved files were read with pypdf (`/Annots`, `/Rect`, `/Contents`, `/AP`, `/Popup`, the number of `startxref`s).
- How it was driven:
  - CDP clicks, mouse drags, keys and evaluations through the `webview` MCP server.
  - Emulated forced colours (not a real high-contrast theme).
  - 25 screenshots, some of them of saved files in headless Edge and Chrome. Each browser ran on its own scratch profile and was stopped by PID.
  - Desktop input: none. A CDP Space once landed on the title bar's Minimize while the keyboard had left the terminal. One `ShowWindow` call restored the window, after checking that it belonged to this check's `marshell-app` by PID, path and title.

## Findings

1. **A zoom, a scroll back or a tab switch made a saved tab "unsaved", and Ctrl+Z then took a note away.** pdf.js puts a highlight's undo step on the stack each time a page's layer takes it (`onceAdded`), and a page drawn again has new layers. Fixed in `d58cbe85`: a note already held that comes back to its page adds no step (`readdGuard_*`, failing first). Live: a zoom, a scroll back and a tab switch stay saved, and Ctrl+Z after a zoom undoes the real note.
2. **Find's Highlight moved the keyboard to the new note**, which broke a run through the matches. Fixed in `58c91d67`: find keeps the keyboard on its button (`keepingFocus_*`, failing first). Live: the keyboard stays on Highlight through runs of Previous and Highlight.
3. **"Show comment" did nothing**, so a comment couldn't be read or edited again from its note. Marshell's comment manager left `toggleCommentPopup` empty. Fixed in `d58cbe85`: the button opens Marshell's popup, and a hover does not (`opensComment_*`, failing first). Live: it opens "Fox comment" and Acrobat's "Check this date", and an edit is saved (pypdf `/Contents`).
4. **The Notes view called every highlight "Highlight editor"**, and a file's highlights showed their whole line or nothing. pdf.js's l10n overwrites the label, and a file has one text item per line. Fixed in `d58cbe85`: a highlight's text is the whole words whose middle lies under it (`highlightText_*`, `textUnder_*`, failing first).
5. **A signed PDF was never blocked.** pdf.js gives every signature field the value null, so the check on field values never fired. Fixed in `21adbc4b`: the check uses pdf.js's `getSignatures()` (`notesBlocked_signed`, failing first; the test's stand-in now behaves as pdf.js does).
6. **On a page with `/Rotate`, the text layer and the notes lay upright on the turned page** (an old fault, which notes make worse). pdf.js marks its layers with `data-main-rotation` and leaves the turn to its stylesheet, which Marshell never had. Find's match, a selection and every note landed where the page would be unturned, and the saved notes were in the wrong place in Edge too. Fixed in `57e7494a`: the turn rules sit in `PdfView.css`'s block adapted from pdf.js, and notes turn about their own corner (`turnedPages.test.ts`, 10 tests, failing first). Live: on `rotated-2.pdf` a free text lands at the click and reads across, a highlight covers the matched words, and Edge draws all three notes where they were put.
7. **A PDF tab left within 1.5 s of a note lost its notes once its panel was unloaded.** The hidden tab's draft timer ran a save while no page was drawn, pdf.js's end of editing threw, and no draft went out. Fixed in `96e67a30`: with no page drawn there is no drawing to end, and a draft that failed is taken again when the tab goes or the window loses focus (tests in `layerInView` and `createNotesRunner`, failing first). Live: `loss-5.pdf`'s draft went out 1.47 s after the note while hidden, and the note came back after the unload. Notes made before this fix (`loss-1`, `loss-3`, `loss-4`, `disk-c`, `disk-d`) were lost, and the strip's dot outlived them.
8. **Reload, Overwrite, the deleted bar's Save and the save line's Undo dropped the keyboard to the page body**, because the pressed button went with its bar. Fixed in `44c6d1f6`: every bar button puts the keyboard on the pages first (`notesButtons`, failing first).
9. **A PDF deleted while its tab was unloaded, or across a restart, showed only "This file was deleted."** The preview looked for a text draft only, so the PDF's draft was stranded. Fixed in `86f0411a`: the PDF view opens on the draft with the deleted bar (Preview "opens the PDF view on a PDF that was already gone, when its notes are kept in a draft", failing first). Live: `del-1.pdf` showed its highlight under "del-1.pdf was deleted. Your notes aren't saved.", and Save wrote it back with the highlight.
10. **A mouse drag let go in the blank past a line's end selected on to the page's last lines** (an old fault since the text layer came in, which notes make worse). The text layer had pdf.js's `endOfContent` styles but not the block itself, so a drag let go between lines landed on the layer. Highlight then wrote a 14-line highlight for 4 lines. With the block put in by hand, the same drag selected 3 lines; without it, 15. Fixed in `09863bce`: each text layer ends with the block, marked "selecting" from a press until the pointer comes up or the window loses focus (`pdfjs.test.ts`, three tests, failing first).

## Leads (not fixed)

- After the first Save as a copy the keyboard was on the body. Two traced retries put it on the copy's pages at 35 to 44 ms. This may be a race between `focusPane`'s frame and React's commit when `openInPane` runs after an `await` (shared code, older than this slice).
- A file held with share None reads as "was deleted", because the core's `/v1/files/stat` answers 404 when it can't open a file. The bar clears when the lock ends. The fix belongs in the core's stat code, outside this slice.
- The restarted window came up minimized after a quit from a window restored with `SW_SHOWNOACTIVATE`. Not checked with a normally restored window. Window state, outside this slice.
- A stat poll sent before a save and answered after it could reload the file as it was before the save (Task 4's ruling). Eight watched cycles didn't show it.

## Minors (not fixed)

1. An undo that brings back a deleted note announces nothing.
2. "Updated" flashes in the pane's header after the tab's own save.
3. While a note stays selected, Enter on the pages does nothing, and Space still places a free text box.
4. The Notes view shows a long comment whole. The plan clamps only the quoted text.
5. pdf.js's writer: editing the comment on a file's note writes a new `/Popup` and leaves the old one in `/Annots`, hidden.
6. pdf.js adds an arrow move's undo step 1 s after the last arrow key, so a drag undone within that second loses its redo. Two drags in a row undo and redo fully.
