## Findings

1. **A session reached with its panel expanded left the keyboard on the page.** Ctrl+Shift+1 from session 2, and a sidebar click, ended on `BODY`: the selected terminal is still drawn while the layout moves (200 ms), takes the keyboard, and is hidden when the motion ends. The rule that moves a lost keyboard into the expanded panel had already run. Fixed in `91f8aa66`: the window tells the app when the terminals are hidden (`onTerminalsCovered`), and the rule runs again then, and after every "back to the terminal" (`focusToken`: a launcher or a sheet that closed). Failing first: "a keyboard left on a covered terminal goes into the expanded panel, however the session was reached" (`LiveApp.test.tsx`) and "tells the app once the pane has covered the terminals and they are hidden" (`MainWindow.test.tsx`). The whole-branch review found the same fault from the code.
2. **The cursor's release on the session's own row ended the expansion,** against the slice's decision that picking the own row leaves it. Fixed in `13424d65` with the review's fix pass: the machine's `select-pane` says when it came from a row, and `releaseOutcome` keeps the panel and puts the keyboard in it. Failing first: `releaseOutcome` (`paneCommands.test.ts`) and "selects the pane of a session that is on screen when its sidebar row is clicked" (`machine.test.ts`).
3. **Under the shell panel's expanded panel, going to a session showed nothing.** The shell panel owns the pane while its terminal has the keyboard, and its expanded panel covers the main area, so the session's terminal could not take the keyboard and the pane stayed the shell's. Fixed in `13424d65`: a session's terminal asked for, or another session selected, hands the pane to the session, and the shell keeps its expansion (`handsToSession`). Seen after the fix: Ctrl+Shift+3 from the shell panel's terminal gave session 3 its panels beside its terminal with the keyboard in it, and the shell's Files was expanded again when the shell panel's terminal took the keyboard back.
4. **The system's "Open" dialog came up once, on the desktop.** The check tried to answer `pick_file` from the page; `window.__TAURI_INTERNALS__.invoke` is read-only, so "Browse…" from the palette opened the real dialog, which took the foreground. It was closed with `WM_CLOSE` sent to its handle after checking that the window was the check's own app's (class `#32770`); nothing was picked, and the panel was as before. Not a product fault. A later check that needs "Browse…" should stop at the palette row.
5. **Pressing Expand a second time puts the keyboard in the terminal,** not back on the button: the terminal takes it when it is shown again, as it does when a covering pane hides. Left as built.
6. **An ended session's terminal doesn't take the keyboard.** After the restart, Ctrl+Shift+2 to session 2 ("Ended last time") left the keyboard on the page. The slice doesn't touch this; not fixed.
