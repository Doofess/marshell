## Findings

1. **A filling pane squeezed into a 1 px track while it slid in or out.** With no session, the grid kept three tracks for about 200 ms of the pane's motion while no terminal node was drawn, so the pane sat in the middle track. Fixed in `bd9f1343`: `emptySlot` in `paneLayout.ts` holds the terminal's slot while a filling pane moves, with four failing-first tests in `paneLayout.test.ts`. Seen live after the fix: the pane slid from 912 px down to 0 on close, and from 22 px up to 912 on open.
2. **The panel's shell was never replaced after `exit`.** `TerminalView`'s `onExit` wrote the exit line but never called the `onExited` prop that Task 6 added, so `LiveApp` never learnt the shell had ended. Fixed in `5e8a1395`: `processExited` in `terminal/processExit.ts` calls it once the exit line is written, with two failing-first tests in `processExit.test.ts`. Item 12 passed after it.
3. **Closing sessions logged 404s from their panes.** Closing sessions 2 and 3 through the API while their panes were drawn logged 404s for `tree`, `changes` and `github` on the closed tab ids, and two pty sockets closed before connecting. Nothing showed in the window. Not fixed; it goes to the leftovers issue.
4. **The launcher hides a page.** When the last session closed, the launcher opened, and as an overlay it hid the shell's page; the page came back when the launcher closed. That is the overlay rule working, not a fault.
5. **Not covered.** Whether closing Settings, the Doctor or a peek gives the pane back to the session (they focus the session's terminal) was not tried; the palette's Esc and a palette command did not take the pane from the shell.
